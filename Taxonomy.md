Event - A time delimitted gathering of people at a location, that may require tickets, directions, instructions or more. Large scale events may have their own "events" within them. For the purposes of making this app, an "Event" is an atomic unit. While Festivals / Weekends / Fiestas may be events in their own right, we treat these larger events by allowing users to create dedicated calendars that capture which events they are interested in attending.

Calendar - A collection of events, potentially itself set between two time boundaries, used to capture events that occur on the same Holiday, Festival, Trip, Weeekend. Can also be a continuous calendar to capture events as they appear. In the context of this app, a calendar is a space where users can invite other users to add events, add their attendance to events and more. 

App Calendar - Used specifically to refer to calendars within the UCA App.

Native Calendar - Used specifically to refer to someone's existing calendars they may use (Such as the iOS Native Calendar or Google Calendar for example)

Fest / Festival / Line-up (Promoter) - A professionally curated event that may have multiple events within it, such as a festival with multiple artists performing at different times and locations. These are systemically captured so that they may be easily imported into user's calendars

Promoter - A professional event organiser who curates large scale Festivals, Conventions, Expositions, Gatherings and more.

User - A person who has many places to be and many places to see, and in need of an app to organise their time among friends.
